\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}
All models are implemented in PyTorch 2.11 (CUDA 12.8) and trained on a single RTX 5070 (12\,GB) shared by up to three jobs.
A final run trains for 7 to 32 minutes depending on the model, and the whole study (120 tuning trials, 30 comparison runs and 80 augmentation runs) took about one day of wall time.
Every run writes a JSON file with its configuration, seed, code commit, data checksums and all metrics, and every number in this paper is generated from those files by scripts in the repository.
The 110 final checkpoints were re-scored per item for the paired statistics; a stored model that did not reproduce its logged score would be flagged, and exactly one of 110 did (\Cref{sec:limits}).

\textbf{Reproduction check.}
Before any new experiment we verified our implementation against published numbers.
The five-seed mean development CER of our tuned BiLSTM is 8.39--8.43, matching Roark et al.'s 8.4~\cite{roark2020dakshina}, and the tuned 3M Transformer reaches 8.49, between their 9.4 and Kirov et al.'s 8.2~\cite{kirov2024context}.
The untuned reproduction-gate runs (one seed, greedy) gave 8.95 and 9.38.
Our top-1 accuracy (63.3\%) is below the published 65.3\% for the BiLSTM; we attribute this to differences in how multiple references and attestation counts are scored and do not hide it.
