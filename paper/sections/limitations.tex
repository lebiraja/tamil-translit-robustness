\section{Limitations}
Dakshina is formal text romanized on request, not chat; we therefore speak of informal \emph{romanized} Tamil and test on separate real-typing sets.
Synthetic noise is a model of typing, and its realism is tested rather than assumed.
Back-transliterated training pairs inherit the errors of the model that made them.
The real-chat set is small, labelled by a language model and verified by the authors, and comes from a few private conversations.
The team-typed set has few typists and was typed from Tamil-script prompts, which may be more careful than spontaneous chat.
Vowel-dropped inputs can be genuinely ambiguous, so we report top-$k$ accuracy as well as top-1.
