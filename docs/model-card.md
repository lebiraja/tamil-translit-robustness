# Model card: Tanglish to Tamil transliterator (v2, deployed)

- **File:** `models/v2-ft-transformer-3m.pt` (12.6 MB, single file, weights only; gitignored; sha256 `b88cfe4d...`). **Hugging Face (public, standalone repo with model card, config and minimal inference code): `lebiraja/tamil-tanglish-translit-v2`**, uploaded 2026-10-22. Format `{model, kw, arch, vocab}`; load with `translit.infer.Transliterator`.
- **Architecture:** character-level pre-LN Transformer encoder-decoder, 3 layers per side, 4 heads, d_model 192, ff 768, 3,153,844 parameters. All parameters trainable during fine-tuning; no adapters, LoRA or frozen layers; nothing to merge.
- **Lineage:** Dakshina-trained A4 Transformer (`c2-transformer-A4-s1`, 5-seed A4 condition: Tamil-phonological noise + 123,488 sampled back-transliterations) -> full fine-tune (`scripts/finetune.py name=v2-ft`, run `results/final/v2-ft-s0.json`, commit in the JSON).
- **Fine-tuning data (2,405,783 pairs per epoch):** Dakshina train x3 (204,654), A3 back-transliterations (123,488), Aksharantar train (2,077,641 pairs after dropping IndicCorp rows scored below -0.10 and any Dakshina dev/test or independent-test word); online noise from all six operators on 50% of items. Adam, lr 1e-4 cosine, 500 warmup, batch 512, label smoothing 0.1, 5 epochs, best epoch by mean of clean and MIX 0.3 dev CER; 20 minutes on an RTX 5070.
- **Input / output:** one lowercase Latin word -> top-5 Tamil words (beam 10). Sentences are split into Latin words converted one at a time (`Transliterator.sentence`); frequent English words are kept as typed by the demo filter (`is_english`).
- **Results (test unless stated):** dev CER 8.56; test CER 8.93, top-1 65.3%, top-5 84.8%; AK-all CER 10.76; mixed noise p=0.3 CER 17.77 (A4 parent 21.80); keyboard slips p=0.3 15.40 (23.04). The noise suites are no longer held-out for this model (it trained on all six operators). Preliminary real-chat check (370 words, model-made labels, unverified): CER 24.9, top-1 39.7%, top-5 71.1% (A4 parent 25.4, 40.5%, 68.6%).
- **Intended use:** typing aid and research artifact for informal romanized Tamil at word level. **Not for:** sentence-level translation, English text, other languages, or any decision about people.
- **Known limits:** colloquial spellings (e.g. `poren`, `enna`) often come out in literary form; vowel-dropped words are ambiguous (check alternatives); no sentence context; English filter is a frequency heuristic; trained on mined and Wikipedia-style romanizations, not on verified chat text; machine-made synthetic pairs in the mixture.
- **Previous model (v1, fallback):** `models/v1-a4-transformer-3m.pt`, same architecture, test CER 9.02, MIX 0.3 CER 21.80.
